EduAssetVerify

Constraint-Grounded Verification of AI-Generated Visual Assets for Educational Games

A picture can look right and still teach the wrong concept, like a fraction drawn with unequal parts. EduAssetVerify asks a vision-language model (VLM) only what an image contains, never the expected answer; it measures the geometry itself, and three-valued rules decide every requirement, sending unclear cases to a person instead of guessing.

Results at a glance

About these results

The assets

The assets are procedurally rendered benchmark images, not outputs of an image generator. Before any verifier ran, an independent script recomputed every label from the pixels, and an item was kept only if both answers agreed. The benchmark has 1,020 images: 120 for development, which fixed prompts and thresholds, and 900 for testing under four conditions (in-domain, unseen templates, two-operator errors, and four unseen styles).

Images are shown losslessly at their native 512 × 512 resolution: the exact pixels the verifiers saw.

The records

Verdicts, evidence graphs and reports are read from the experiment records of the paper (three independent passes per method and backbone); the judges' answers come from the response cache of the same runs. Measurement overlays are recomputed with the system's own operators and match the recorded values. No human rater was involved.

Where the judges win

Once abstentions count as errors, every judge is more accurate than EduAssetVerify under Gemini 3.5 Flash, and EduAssetVerify is more accurate than every judge under Claude Sonnet 5.

The rule-only validator, which uses the same evidence without the semantic verifier and abstention, also accepted no violated asset and was more accurate than the full method under both backbones. Removing the semantic verifier left FAR at zero and moved FRR by less than half a point; abstention prevented no false acceptance but turned false rejections into reviews.

What this page is not

The simulation, the game screens and the playground are illustrations built on the recorded verdicts and on the system's rules. Nothing here was tested with learners, and no statement on this page is about learning outcomes.
